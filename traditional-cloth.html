Design concept (short description): This 2D WebGL pattern is inspired by Ethiopian Tibeb textiles: a central woven band with repeating geometric motifs (diamonds, chevrons, and stepped crosses) framed by border stripes. The pattern is built from basic geometric primitives (triangles/lines) and arranged through transformations (translation, rotation, and scaling) to achieve symmetry and repetition. Interactivity lets you explore variation in density, motif scale, palette, and rotation—similar to adjusting weave rhythm and ornament balance in real cloth design.
